## Deviations and assumptions
- `rankResults` takes the `ranking` block as its `config` and validates it at runtime (it may come from jsonb). Returns an `EngineResult`: errors for an invalid config, repeated student, non-integer roll, unknown subject key, and a missing section when the `section` scope is on and the student is ranked.
- Ranks are computed on the stored hundredths (`total`, `gpa`), not on exact fractions; two students whose totals differ only below the stored rounding are tied.
- `outcome` orders `passed` before `failed`; `withheld` and `absent` are never ranked even if `include_failed` is true.
- `subject:<id>_desc` compares the subject's total marks; a student without that subject counted ranks last on that key. Naming a subject no student has is an error.
- "Top N" is defined as every student with `class_rank <= N`, so ties can return more than N. Spec says only "top N"; please confirm.
- Statistics: pass rate is `passed / appeared` (absent students excluded), a subject's averages are over students who appeared, and failed students count in the overall averages with their stored total and GPA (0.00 by default). Spec gives no definition; these follow common school practice and need confirming, and Munshi parity would settle them.
- `classStats` returns a plain value (never errors); callers pass each student once.
